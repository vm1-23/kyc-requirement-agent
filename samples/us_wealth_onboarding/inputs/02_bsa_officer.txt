Dana: Our CIP requires that we collect name, date of birth, address and SSN or TIN before opening any account.
Identity must be verified through a documentary or non-documentary method within 5 business days of account opening.
For trusts, we must identify the trustees and verify the identity of each trustee.
All new clients must be checked against the OFAC SDN list before the account is opened.
SAR decisions must be documented and SARs filed within 30 days of detection.
