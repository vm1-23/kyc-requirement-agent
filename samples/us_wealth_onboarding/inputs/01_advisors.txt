Advisor workshop notes
- Advisors want to start an application on behalf of a client and send it to the client to finish and e-sign.
- Clients should be able to link their external bank account for funding during onboarding.
- The application must collect the client's employment status, net worth and investment objectives for suitability.
- Accounts for existing clients should open instantly without repeating identity checks.
- New accounts must be created in the portfolio accounting mainframe, which only accepts files twice a day.
