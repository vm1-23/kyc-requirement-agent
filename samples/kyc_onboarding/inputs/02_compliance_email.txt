Subject: KYC must-haves for the digital onboarding project

Team,
The system must screen every applicant against UN, domestic and internal sanctions lists before the account is opened.
All customers must be screened for PEP status and PEP relationships must be approved by a senior compliance officer.
The system shall assign every customer a low, medium or high risk rating based on the approved risk-scoring model.
High-risk customers must be manually reviewed and go through enhanced due diligence with source of funds evidence.
Periodic re-KYC must be scheduled every 2 years for high-risk, 8 years for medium-risk and 10 years for low-risk customers.
KYC records must be retained for at least 5 years after the account is closed.
KYC records of new customers must be uploaded to CKYCR within the regulatory timeline.
Where video KYC is used, the session must include liveness check, geo-tagging and face match by a trained official, and the recording must be stored securely.
Staff must be able to escalate suspicious applications to the Principal Officer for STR filing without alerting the customer.
