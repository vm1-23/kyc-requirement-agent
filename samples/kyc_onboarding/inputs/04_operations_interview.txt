Interviewer: How does KYC operations work today?
Rahul: Currently our team manually checks about 4,000 applications a day from branches, and the backlog is often 2 days.
Any manual override of an automated KYC decision must go through maker-checker approval with a reason.
Potential sanctions matches need a case management queue where analysts can review evidence and close alerts with comments.
We need the review queue to show cases sorted by risk and SLA age.
Ops needs a daily MIS report of applications approved, rejected and pending, by channel.
If the e-KYC vendor is down, applications should be queued and customers notified, not rejected.
Customer data must be retained for 10 years after the account is closed.
