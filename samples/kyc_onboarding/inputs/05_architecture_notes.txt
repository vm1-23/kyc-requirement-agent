Architecture constraints
The legacy core banking system only creates accounts through a nightly batch file; real-time account creation is not available until the CBS upgrade next year.
The onboarding platform must integrate with the core banking system through an asynchronous adapter and report account status back to the customer.
The onboarding APIs must handle a peak of 200 applications per minute during campaigns.
The onboarding service must have 99.9% monthly availability with an RTO of 4 hours and RPO of 15 minutes.
All integrations with vendors (e-KYC, video KYC, screening) must go through the API gateway.
