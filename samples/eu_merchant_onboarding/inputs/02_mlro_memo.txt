Memo: Merchant onboarding AML requirements
1. The system must identify and verify all beneficial owners holding 25% or more of the merchant entity.
2. Every merchant, director and beneficial owner must be screened against EU and UN sanctions lists and PEP databases.
3. Merchants in high-risk sectors such as gambling or crypto must undergo enhanced due diligence before activation.
4. Each merchant must receive a risk score that is reviewed at least annually for high-risk merchants.
5. All onboarding records must be kept for 5 years after the merchant relationship ends.
6. The system shall allow analysts to file suspicious activity reports to the FIU.
